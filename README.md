## About WakilAI

WakilAI is a legal intelligence platform built for organizations and professionals working with Uzbekistan's legal system.

We combine structured legal data, multi-stage AI reasoning, evidence retrieval, document intelligence, and enterprise deployment infrastructure to help legal teams research, analyze, draft, and work with law more efficiently.

WakilAI is designed around a simple principle:

> **A legal AI system should not just generate an answer. It should be able to show what that answer is based on.**

Our platform is built for individuals, law firms, enterprises, legal-aid organizations, and public institutions that require more than a general-purpose AI assistant.

## Built for Legal Precision

General-purpose AI systems optimize for producing useful language.

WakilAI is designed around a different requirement: **legal answers must be grounded, traceable, and contextually correct.**

Our legal intelligence pipeline separates retrieval, evidence evaluation, and answer generation rather than treating them as a single generation step.

    A[User or Enterprise System] --> B[Context & Intent Analysis]
    B --> C[Domain & Retrieval Strategy]
    C --> D[Legal Evidence Retrieval]
    D --> E[Evidence Quality Gate]
    E --> F[Grounded Legal Synthesis]
    F --> G[Citations & Structured Output]
